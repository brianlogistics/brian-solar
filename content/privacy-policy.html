Privacy Policy

Brian Solar & Electrical

Registered Business Name: BRIAN SOLAR & ELECTRICAL

Last Updated: 20 November 2026

1. Introduction

Brian Solar & Electrical respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Kenyan data protection laws, including the Data Protection Act, 2019.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared, and the rights available to you.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quotation or use our website, we may collect:

  • Your name and telephone number.
  • Your email address, where provided.
  • Your town, location or project site details.
  • The service you are interested in: Solar, Electrical, CCTV or Electric Fence.
  • Your estimated budget and project requirements, where provided.
  • Messages and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Relevant technical information, such as the page visited, referral source, browser information and enquiry submission date and time.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, we may also receive information and attachments you choose to send through that service.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

  • Complete a quotation or enquiry form on our website.
  • Contact us by telephone, email or WhatsApp.
  • Communicate with us about a quotation or project.
  • Interact with our website and its security or technical features.

Our website uses Google reCAPTCHA to help identify and prevent spam and automated submissions. Google may process technical information in connection with this security service.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Assessing project requirements.
  • Preparing and communicating quotations.
  • Arranging site assessments and installations.
  • Providing Solar, Electrical, CCTV and Electric Fence services.
  • Managing customer relationships and project records.
  • Following up on quotations and customer requests.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing spam.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not use enquiry information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

5. How Enquiry Information Is Processed

Information submitted through our website quotation forms may be processed using Google Apps Script and Google Sheets for enquiry recording and customer relationship management.

The website may also open WhatsApp to allow you to communicate with our business directly. You may choose whether to send the prepared WhatsApp message.

Google, WhatsApp and other service providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our website, record enquiries, communicate with customers or deliver requested services.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights and prevent fraud or misuse.

Where personal information is transferred outside Kenya, we will take the steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure appropriate safeguards or obtain consent where required.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Our intended retention approach is:

  • Unsuccessful enquiries: up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, followed by a review for deletion.
  • Completed projects: relevant records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, warranties, accounting, dispute resolution and legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or anonymise it, unless continued retention is required or permitted by law.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is used.
  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain processing of your information.
  • Request deletion or erasure where legally applicable.
  • Request restriction of processing or data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya if you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services, including WhatsApp and social media platforms.

We do not control the privacy practices of those external services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
