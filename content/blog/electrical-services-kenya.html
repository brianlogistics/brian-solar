Residential electrical services

A residential electrical installation should be planned around the building layout, expected appliances, lighting requirements and available electrical supply.

Work may include wiring routes, socket outlets, switches, lighting points, a distribution board and suitable circuit protection. For renovations or additions, the existing installation should be assessed before new circuits or equipment are connected.

Commercial electrical services

Shops, offices and other business premises may have different electrical requirements from a typical home. Equipment loads, lighting, operating hours and the layout of the premises all affect the work involved.

Discuss the intended use of the property and the equipment to be connected when requesting an assessment. This helps identify the information needed to plan the installation or upgrade.

Electrical safety and good installation practice

Electrical work should be planned and carried out with attention to the condition of the installation, suitable cable and accessory selection, circuit protection, earthing and the requirements applicable to the project.

  • Have electrical faults investigated rather than repeatedly resetting a tripping circuit.
  • Do not use damaged sockets, switches, cables or exposed conductors.
  • Avoid overloading sockets and extension leads.
  • Ensure circuit protection is appropriate for the circuit and connected load.
  • Have alterations assessed before adding high-power appliances or new circuits.

If you notice burning smells, sparking, overheating or exposed live wiring, keep people away from the affected area and arrange for the supply to be isolated safely by a competent person. Do not attempt repairs on live equipment.

Frequently asked questions

Can you investigate electrical faults?

You can contact us about faults such as repeated circuit tripping, damaged accessories, loss of power to a circuit or other electrical problems. The cause should be assessed before repairs are recommended.

Can an existing electrical installation be upgraded?

Yes, upgrades and alterations can be discussed. The existing installation, electrical demand and proposed additions should be assessed to determine the work required.

What information is needed for an electrical quotation?

Provide your location, property type, description of the work and any relevant photos or drawings. For a fault, describe what is happening and when it occurs. An assessment may be needed before the scope and cost can be confirmed.
