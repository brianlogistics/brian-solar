Solar & Electrical Services in Nairobi

Brian Solar & Electrical provides solar and electrical services in Nairobi, with additional CCTV and electric fence solutions for property security. We plan projects for homes, offices, shops, schools, farms and institutions around their energy needs, equipment and intended use.

Our Services in Nairobi

  • Solar: Solar PV panels, inverter systems, battery backup, solar water heating and suitable borehole solarisation projects.
  • Electrical: Electrical wiring, installations, upgrades, repairs and improvements for residential and commercial properties.
  • CCTV: CCTV camera installation and system planning for homes, offices, shops and institutions.
  • Electric Fence: Electric fence installation and perimeter security solutions for suitable properties.

Solar Systems for Homes and Businesses

Solar system planning starts with the appliances and equipment that need power, their usage patterns and the backup time required. Offices, retail premises, schools and homes can have different daytime and evening loads, so the system should be considered against the actual operating requirements.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical work is planned around the existing installation, equipment requirements and intended use of the property. Installation, repair and upgrade needs are assessed before the appropriate scope of work is agreed.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera positions, coverage areas, entrances and important spaces. Electric fence planning takes account of the property's boundary, access gates and the areas requiring protection.

Where a property needs more than one service, the requirements can be discussed together so that related work is considered in the overall project scope.

Planning Your Nairobi Project

When requesting a quotation, share your area in Nairobi, property type and the services required. For solar projects, provide an appliance or equipment list, expected usage and backup needs. For electrical work, photographs or details of the existing installation can help. For CCTV and electric fence projects, a property layout, entrances and perimeter details are useful. A site assessment may be needed before confirming the final scope and cost.

Projects in Nairobi and Across Kenya

We arrange projects in Nairobi as part of our Kenya-wide service area. Site visits and installation arrangements are agreed according to the location and scope of the work.
