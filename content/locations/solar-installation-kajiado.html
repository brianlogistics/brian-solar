Solar Power for Homes, Farms and Remote Properties

Solar requirements can differ significantly between a household, a farm and a commercial property. System planning considers the equipment being powered, daily energy demand, operating hours, backup requirements and available installation space.

Where water pumping is required, the pump's specifications, water source, pumping requirements and expected operating conditions should be assessed before selecting a solar pumping arrangement.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical work is assessed according to the existing installation, wiring condition, equipment requirements and improvements needed. The project scope is established before recommending suitable installation, repair or upgrade work.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera coverage, entrances, monitoring areas and suitable equipment positions. Electric fence projects take account of boundary dimensions, access points and the perimeter areas requiring protection.

Planning Your Kajiado Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar projects, an equipment list, expected operating hours and any water pumping requirements are useful. For electrical and security work, photographs of the existing installation, property layout or boundary can help explain the project. Site access and installation conditions may need to be assessed before the final scope and cost are confirmed.
