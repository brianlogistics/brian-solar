Solar Installation Planning in Machakos

Solar system planning considers the appliances and equipment to be powered, their daily usage, operating hours and the backup required. Farms and businesses may also have pumps, machinery or other equipment that needs to be included when assessing system capacity.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical work is planned around the property's existing wiring, electrical equipment, intended loads and the improvements required. The condition of the installation and the scope of work help determine the appropriate approach.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera positions, entrances, access routes and the areas that need monitoring. Electric fence projects take account of boundary layout, gates, perimeter length and the property's security requirements.

What to Prepare Before Requesting a Quote

Share your project location, property type and the services required. For solar, provide an appliance or equipment list, expected usage and any water-pumping requirements. For electrical work, photos or details of the existing installation can help explain the scope. For CCTV and electric fence projects, a property layout, entrance locations and perimeter details are useful. A site assessment may be needed before confirming the final work and cost.
