Borehole Solarisation and Water Pumping

A solar-powered borehole project should be planned around the water requirement, pump specifications, pumping hours and the required water delivery. The borehole depth, lifting requirements, pipework and available sunlight also affect system design.

These details should be assessed before selecting the solar array, pump equipment and any supporting components. A system should not be sized from panel count alone.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical work is planned around the existing installation, equipment requirements and intended use of the property. Installation, repair and upgrade needs are assessed before the appropriate scope is agreed.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera positions, coverage areas and access points. Electric fence planning takes account of the property boundary, gates and the areas requiring perimeter protection.

Planning Your Kitui Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type, required services and relevant equipment details. For borehole solarisation, include pump specifications, borehole depth, water demand and expected pumping hours where available. Photographs or a property layout can help explain electrical and security requirements. A site assessment may be needed before confirming the final scope and cost.
