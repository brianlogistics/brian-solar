Solar Installation Planning in Nakuru

A suitable solar system depends on the property's energy requirements. We consider the appliances and equipment to be powered, daily consumption, operating schedules, backup expectations and available installation space.

For agricultural properties, water pumping and other equipment loads may need separate consideration. Pump specifications and expected water demand should be reviewed before designing a solar pumping system.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera coverage, entrances, lighting conditions and suitable equipment positions. Electric fence projects take account of perimeter layout, access gates and the areas requiring protection.

Planning Your Nakuru Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar projects, an equipment list, operating hours and backup needs are useful. For agricultural and water-pumping projects, include pump specifications and expected water demand if available. For electrical and security work, photographs of the existing installation, property layout or perimeter can help explain the requirements. A site assessment may be needed before the final scope and cost are confirmed.
