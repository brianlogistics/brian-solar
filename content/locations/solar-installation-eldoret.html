Solar Power for Residential and Commercial Properties

Solar system design begins with an understanding of the loads that need to operate. Appliance ratings, daily usage, operating hours and the required backup period help determine the appropriate system configuration.

Businesses and institutions may have different daytime and evening loads. These patterns should be reviewed before selecting solar panels, inverter capacity and battery storage.

Agricultural and Water Pumping Requirements

Where a project involves agricultural equipment or water pumping, the required operating capacity and expected usage should be established first. Pump specifications, water requirements and site conditions influence the design of a suitable solar pumping system.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera coverage, property access and suitable equipment positions. Electric fence projects take account of boundary dimensions, gates and the perimeter areas requiring protection.

Planning Your Eldoret Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar projects, provide an appliance or equipment list, expected operating hours and any water pumping requirements. For electrical and security work, photographs of the existing installation, property layout or perimeter can help explain the project. A site assessment may be necessary before confirming the final scope and cost.
