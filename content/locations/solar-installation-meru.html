Solar Systems for Homes and Farms in Meru

Solar requirements depend on the equipment being powered and how it is used. We consider daily energy consumption, appliance ratings, operating hours, backup expectations and available installation space when planning a system.

For farms and properties that require water pumping, the pump's power requirements, expected water demand and operating conditions should be assessed before selecting the solar equipment.

Electrical Installations and Improvements

Electrical work is planned around the existing installation, the equipment involved and the improvements required. The project scope should be established before work begins so that the proposed installation addresses the property's actual requirements.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers the areas that need monitoring, entrances, camera positions and available equipment locations. Electric fence planning takes account of the property's boundaries, gates and the perimeter sections requiring protection.

Planning Your Meru Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar projects, an appliance or equipment list, operating hours and expected backup needs are useful. For farm and water-pumping projects, include the pump specifications and expected water demand if available. For electrical and security work, photographs of the existing installation, property layout or boundary can help explain the requirements. A site assessment may be needed before the final scope and cost are confirmed.
