Solar and Electrical Planning in Coastal Conditions

Coastal sites need practical consideration when selecting and positioning outdoor equipment. Exposure to moisture and salt-laden air can affect metal components and electrical connections over time. Equipment selection, suitable enclosures, cable entries, mounting arrangements and maintenance access should therefore be considered during project planning.

Solar system sizing should also reflect the property's actual energy use, available installation space and desired backup. Hotels and commercial premises may have different operating loads from a residential property.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical installations, repairs and upgrades are planned around the existing wiring, equipment requirements and intended use of the property. The work is scoped after considering the installation and improvements required.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV camera positions should take account of entrances, access routes and the areas the property owner wants to monitor. Electric fence planning should consider the boundary layout, access gates and the intended perimeter protection.

Planning Your Mombasa Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar projects, an equipment list, operating hours and backup needs help establish the energy requirements. For electrical work, details or photographs of the existing installation are useful. For CCTV and electric fence projects, share the property layout, entrances and perimeter details where possible. Coastal exposure and equipment placement can also be considered during site assessment before the final scope and cost are confirmed.
