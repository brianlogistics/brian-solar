Solar Installation Planning in Kitengela

The right solar system depends on the appliances and equipment you use, daily energy consumption, required backup time and whether water pumps also need to operate. Reviewing these requirements before selecting equipment helps shape a system around actual usage rather than an assumed system size.

When requesting a quotation, share your property type, location, equipment list and expected usage. For electrical and security projects, photographs or a property layout can help explain the work required. A site assessment may be needed before confirming the final scope and cost.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

CCTV and Electric Fence Security
