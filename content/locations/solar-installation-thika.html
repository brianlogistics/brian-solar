Solar Systems for Homes and Businesses in Thika

Solar system planning starts with understanding the equipment and appliances that need power. Daily energy consumption, operating hours, backup expectations and site conditions all help determine a suitable system configuration.

For workshops and commercial properties, equipment loads and operating schedules should be considered before selecting an inverter, battery capacity and solar panel arrangement.

Electrical Installation, Repairs and Upgrades

Electrical work is planned around the existing wiring and installation, the equipment in use and the property's electrical requirements. The scope may include new installations, repairs, upgrades or improvements, depending on the condition of the existing system.

CCTV and Electric Fence Security

CCTV planning considers camera positions, entrances, access routes and the areas that need monitoring. Electric fence projects take account of the property's boundary layout, gates, perimeter length and security requirements.

Planning Your Thika Project

When requesting a quotation, share your location, property type and the services required. For solar, provide an appliance or equipment list, expected usage and operating hours. For workshop or business installations, equipment loads and schedules are useful. Photos of existing electrical installations, property layouts and perimeter boundaries can help explain electrical and security requirements. A site assessment may be needed before confirming the final scope and cost.
