About Brian Solar & Electrical

Who We Are

Brian Solar & Electrical is a Kenya-based technical services company providing solar, electrical, CCTV, and electric fence solutions for homes, businesses, farms, institutions, and other properties.

Solar is our leading service division. We also provide practical electrical installations and improvements, CCTV systems, and electric fence solutions to help customers manage their energy needs, electrical systems, and property security.

We serve customers across Kenya through a flexible service-area model. Our team discusses each project with the customer and plans the work according to the property's requirements and location.

Our Four Service Divisions

Solar solutions: Solar system design, supply and installation, solar water heating, water pumping, borehole solarisation, lithium battery backup, maintenance, and repairs.

Electrical services: Electrical wiring and installations, upgrades, fault finding, repairs, maintenance, and electrical improvements for properties.

CCTV installation: Camera planning and installation, recorder and storage setup, and remote-viewing configuration where supported by the equipment and network.

Electric fence solutions: Electric fence installation, perimeter planning, system improvements, maintenance, and repairs.

Who We Serve

We work with homeowners, landlords, property developers, farms, shops, offices, businesses, and institutions. Each property has different energy, electrical, and security requirements, so we discuss the intended use and site conditions before recommending a suitable solution.

Whether you are planning a solar system, improving existing electrical installations, adding CCTV coverage, or strengthening perimeter security, we aim to recommend practical work that reflects your needs and budget.

How We Handle Projects

We begin by understanding the customer's requirements and the work involved. Depending on the project, this may include reviewing appliance loads, assessing the property, considering equipment and installation requirements, and discussing suitable options.

Before work is arranged, we discuss the proposed scope, quotation, and relevant project costs. If a site visit or assessment may involve transport or other charges, these are discussed with the customer before the visit is arranged.

Once the scope is agreed, the work proceeds through the relevant stages of supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and handover. Any maintenance or after-sales support arrangements are discussed as part of the project.

Our Commitment to Customers

We aim to provide clear communication, practical recommendations, careful workmanship, and solutions suited to each property's requirements. Our goal is to help customers make informed decisions about their energy, electrical, and security projects.
