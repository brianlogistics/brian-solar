Planning CCTV for Your Property

A useful CCTV system starts with understanding what needs to be monitored. For a home, this may include the main entrance, gate, driveway, compound and other access points. A shop or office may need coverage of entrances, customer areas, stock areas and other appropriate locations. Farms and larger properties may require a different camera layout.

Camera selection should consider the viewing distance, image detail, lighting conditions, mounting position and exposure to weather. Night visibility also depends on the camera's capabilities and the lighting around the monitored area. We can discuss these requirements before recommending an installation approach.

Recording, Storage and Remote Viewing

CCTV recording equipment commonly uses a DVR or NVR, depending on the camera technology and system design. Storage capacity and recording settings affect how much footage can be retained. The appropriate arrangement depends on the number of cameras, recording quality, recording schedule and available storage.

Remote viewing may be available when the cameras, recorder, application and network connection support it. Internet access, equipment compatibility and configuration can affect remote access. We can assess the proposed setup and explain the requirements.

What Affects CCTV Installation Cost?

CCTV installation costs depend on the requirements of each property. Important factors include:

  • The number of cameras and the areas requiring coverage.
  • Camera resolution, night-vision features and other equipment specifications.
  • DVR or NVR selection and the required recording storage.
  • Cable routes, mounting positions and installation complexity.
  • Network requirements and whether remote viewing is needed.
  • Whether the project involves a new installation, repairs or an upgrade.

A property assessment or clear description of the site helps us discuss a suitable system and prepare a more relevant quotation. We do not assume every property needs the same number or type of cameras.
