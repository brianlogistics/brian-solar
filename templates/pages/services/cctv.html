Our CCTV Installation Process

  1. Site Assessment: We review the property and identify important areas requiring surveillance.
  2. System Planning: We recommend suitable camera positions and equipment based on the required coverage.
  3. Installation: Cameras, recording equipment and related components are installed and connected.
  4. Configuration & Testing: The system is configured, tested and checked for proper operation.
  5. Support: We provide guidance on using the installed CCTV system and can assist with maintenance or future upgrades.
