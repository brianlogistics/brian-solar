Solar Installation and Power Solutions

Brian Solar & Electrical designs, supplies and installs solar power systems for homes, businesses, farms and institutions across Kenya. We consider how electricity is used at the property, the equipment that needs power and the conditions at the installation site before recommending a suitable system.

A well-planned solar installation can help reduce reliance on grid electricity, provide backup power where required and support household, business, agricultural and water-pumping applications. The right design depends on the customer's actual energy requirements and priorities.

Solar Solutions for Homeowners

For a home, planning starts with the appliances and circuits that matter most. Lighting, televisions, refrigerators, computers, water pumps and other appliances have different power requirements. We use the intended usage to help determine the inverter capacity, solar panel array and whether battery storage is appropriate.

Choosing the Right Solar System

System sizing considers appliance power ratings, daily electricity consumption, daytime and evening usage, the equipment that must remain powered during an outage and the desired backup duration. Available installation space and the property's electrical arrangement also affect the design. A solar calculator can help with an initial estimate, while a project assessment helps refine the system specification.

What Affects Solar Installation Cost?

The cost of a solar installation depends on the required system capacity, panel and inverter specifications, battery capacity, mounting arrangements, electrical protection, site conditions and installation work. A system designed for essential lighting and selected appliances will differ from one intended to support several high-power appliances or extended backup.

Solar FAQs

How much does solar installation cost in Kenya?

The price depends on the system capacity, panels, inverter, battery storage, mounting requirements, electrical protection and site conditions. Share your requirements with us so we can discuss a suitable system and quotation.

What size solar system does my home need?

The required size depends on the appliances you want to operate, their power ratings, how long they are used and whether you need backup during grid interruptions. A load assessment helps establish the appropriate system capacity.

Can a solar system run a refrigerator and other household appliances?

Yes, a correctly designed system can supply selected household appliances. The inverter, panels and battery arrangement must be matched to the appliances' starting and running power requirements and expected usage.

Do I need batteries with solar panels?

Not every solar installation needs battery storage. Batteries are useful when energy needs to be stored for use later or when backup power is required. The appropriate arrangement depends on the system design and how the property uses electricity.

Can solar power be used for boreholes and water pumping?

Yes. Solar pumping can support borehole water supply, irrigation, livestock and other agricultural or domestic water needs. Pump selection and system design depend on the water demand, pumping depth, required flow and site conditions.

Do you install solar water heaters?

Yes. We provide solar water heating solutions for residential, commercial and institutional hot-water requirements.

Do you provide solar maintenance and repairs?

Yes. We provide maintenance, troubleshooting and repair support for solar panels, inverters, batteries and related equipment.

Do you provide solar installation in Kitengela?

Yes. We undertake residential and commercial solar projects in Kitengela and other parts of Kajiado County.

Which areas of Kenya do you serve?

Brian Solar & Electrical undertakes solar projects across Kenya. Contact us with your project location and requirements so we can discuss the work involved.
