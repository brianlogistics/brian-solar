Planning an Electric Fence for Your Property

Every property has different perimeter requirements. A home may need attention to boundary walls, gates and pedestrian access. A farm or larger property may have longer boundaries, uneven ground, different fence structures and several access points. These details affect the design and scope of the installation.

During planning, we consider the existing boundary structure, the condition of the mounting surface, gate locations, access requirements and the equipment appropriate for the site. The installation must also be planned and completed in accordance with applicable safety requirements.

Our Electric Fence Installation Process

  1. Property Assessment: We discuss the property, perimeter, boundary structure and access points.
  2. Installation Planning: We identify the work required and consider suitable equipment, site conditions and the agreed coverage.
  3. Quotation: We explain the proposed scope and relevant equipment before installation work begins.
  4. Installation: The agreed system and associated components are installed with attention to the property's requirements and safety.
  5. Testing and Handover: The completed installation is checked and tested, and the customer receives relevant operating guidance.
  6. Maintenance Support: We can discuss inspection, troubleshooting, repairs and future upgrades when required.

What Affects Electric Fence Installation Cost?

The cost of an electric fence project depends on the property and the work involved. Important factors include:

  • The total perimeter length and layout.
  • The type and condition of the existing wall, fence or mounting structure.
  • The number and arrangement of gates and access points.
  • Site terrain, accessibility and installation complexity.
  • The equipment and system requirements for the property.
  • Whether the work is a new installation, repair, maintenance task or upgrade.
  • Whether CCTV or other related security work is included in the project.

Sharing the property location, approximate perimeter length, boundary type and any existing system details helps us understand the work and discuss a relevant quotation. A site assessment may be needed to confirm the installation requirements.

Can you install an electric fence on an existing wall or boundary?

Can you repair an existing electric fence?

Yes. We can assess suitable existing installations and discuss troubleshooting and repair work based on the condition of the system.

Do electric fences require maintenance?

Periodic inspection and appropriate maintenance can help identify visible damage, wear and equipment problems. The maintenance requirements depend on the installation and site conditions.

Can electric fencing be combined with CCTV?

Yes. Electric fencing and CCTV can complement one another as part of a wider property security arrangement. We provide both services.

Can gates and access points be included in the planning?
