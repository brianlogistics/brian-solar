Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a solar system cost in Kenya?

The cost depends on your energy needs, system size, equipment, battery capacity and installation requirements. Contact Brian Solar & Electrical to discuss your project and request a quotation.

What size solar system do I need?

The right system depends on your daily electricity consumption, appliances, peak power demand and backup requirements. These details help determine suitable solar panels, an inverter and batteries.

How much battery backup do I need?

Battery capacity depends on the appliances you want to power, their energy consumption and the number of hours of backup required. Your usage requirements help determine a suitable battery configuration.

Do you provide solar installation services in Kitengela?

Yes. Brian Solar & Electrical provides residential and commercial solar installation services in Kitengela, Kajiado County and surrounding areas, subject to project requirements.

Do you provide electrical installation and repair services?

Yes. Our electrical services include wiring, installations, upgrades, maintenance, repairs and troubleshooting for residential and commercial electrical systems.

Do you install and maintain CCTV systems?

Yes. We provide CCTV installation, system configuration and maintenance services. The recommended setup depends on the property, coverage needs and project requirements.

Do you install and maintain electric fences?

Yes. We provide electric fence installation, maintenance and repair services. The appropriate solution depends on the property layout and security requirements.

Do you offer site assessments?

We discuss your project requirements and can arrange an on-site assessment where appropriate. Assessment arrangements depend on the project and location.

Which areas do you serve?

Our service areas include Nairobi, Kiambu, Thika, Kajiado, Kitengela, Machakos, Kitui, Nakuru, Eldoret, Mombasa, Kisumu, Meru and surrounding areas. We also consider projects elsewhere in Kenya. Availability depends on project scope and location.

What happens after I request a quotation?

We review the information you provide and may contact you to clarify your requirements. We then consider the project details and prepare a suitable technical solution and quotation.
