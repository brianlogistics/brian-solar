Electrical Services in Kenya

Brian Solar & Electrical provides practical electrical installation and maintenance services for homes, businesses, institutions and other properties. We focus on safe, properly planned installations and dependable maintenance.

Our Electrical Service Process

  1. Assessment — We assess the property's electrical requirements and existing installation.
  2. Planning — We identify the required work, equipment and installation approach.
  3. Installation or Repair — The agreed electrical work is completed with appropriate care and protection.
  4. Testing — Completed work is checked before handover.
  5. Maintenance Support — Ongoing maintenance and troubleshooting support is available when required.

Who We Serve

Our electrical services are suitable for homeowners, businesses, farms, institutions, offices and other properties requiring installation, repair, maintenance or electrical upgrades.

We also provide electrical work alongside solar installations, helping clients integrate solar generation, inverters, batteries and electrical protection into their property.
