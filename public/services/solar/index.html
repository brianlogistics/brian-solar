Our Solar Installation Process

  1. Site Assessment — We assess the property, energy requirements and installation conditions.
  2. System Design — We size the solar panels, inverter, battery storage and related equipment.
  3. Installation — Equipment is installed with appropriate electrical protection and system configuration.
  4. Commissioning — The completed system is tested and configured before handover.
  5. After-Sales Support — Maintenance, troubleshooting and repair support is available when required.
