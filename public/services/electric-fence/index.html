Our Electric Fence Installation Process

  1. Site Assessment: We assess the perimeter, access points and installation requirements.
  2. System Planning: We determine a practical installation approach based on the property and required perimeter coverage.
  3. Installation: The electric fence system and associated equipment are installed appropriately for the property.
  4. Testing: The completed installation is checked and tested before handover.
  5. Maintenance Support: We can assist with repairs, maintenance and future upgrades.
