About Brian Solar & Electrical

Brian Solar & Electrical is a Kenya-based technical services company providing reliable Solar, Electrical, CCTV, and Electric Fence solutions for homes, businesses, institutions, farms, and other properties.

We help our clients improve energy efficiency, electrical safety, backup power, and property security through professionally designed and installed solutions.

Our services include solar power systems, solar water pumping and borehole solarisation, solar water heating, lithium battery backup systems, electrical wiring and installations, electrical upgrades and maintenance, CCTV installation, and electric fence installation.

From system design and load assessment to installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance, we focus on practical solutions that match each client's requirements and budget.

With hands-on technical experience and a commitment to quality workmanship, Brian Solar & Electrical serves clients across Kenya through a flexible service-area model.

Our Approach

We believe every project should begin with understanding the client's actual needs. That's why we provide site assessments, load analysis, system sizing, professional quotations, installation, testing, commissioning, and after-sales support.

Whether you need to reduce electricity costs, improve your property's electrical system, install backup power, monitor your property with CCTV, or strengthen perimeter security with an electric fence, we provide integrated technical solutions under one trusted brand.
