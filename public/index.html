Frequently Asked Questions

How much does solar cost in Kenya?

Solar system costs in Kenya depend on system size, equipment, battery capacity, installation requirements and the client's energy needs. Contact Brian Solar & Electrical for a site assessment and quotation.

Do you provide solar installation services in Kitengela?

Yes. Brian Solar & Electrical provides residential and commercial solar installation services in Kitengela, Kajiado County and surrounding areas.

How much battery backup do I need?

The battery capacity you need depends on the appliances you want to power, their energy consumption and how many hours of backup you require. We can help assess your needs and recommend a suitable battery configuration.

What size solar system do I need?

The right system size depends on your daily electricity consumption, appliances, peak power demand and backup requirements. A proper assessment helps determine suitable panel, inverter and battery capacities.

Do you offer site assessments?

We discuss your project requirements and can arrange an on-site consultation where appropriate. Assessment arrangements depend on the project and location.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve customers in Nairobi, Kiambu, Thika, Kajiado, Kitengela, Machakos, Kitui, Nakuru, Eldoret, Mombasa, Kisumu, Meru and surrounding areas. Service availability depends on the project requirements and location.

What happens after I request a quotation?

We review the information you provide and may contact you to clarify your requirements. We then assess the project and prepare a suitable technical solution and quotation.
